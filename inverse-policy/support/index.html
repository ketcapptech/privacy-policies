How to Play

  • Tap PLAY to start a run. Your player moves around the ring on its own.
  • Tap anywhere on the screen to switch between the inner and outer lane.
  • Watch which lane each incoming obstacle is on and switch away from it in time. One hit ends the run.
  • Every second you survive earns points. Switch just before an obstacle reaches you for a Perfect bonus, and chain Perfects to build a combo. A very late escape counts as a Near Miss and earns less.
  • The run speeds up over time and obstacles get denser. Watch out for special events such as Overdrive, Reverse, and Glitch Storm.
  • After a crash, tap to retry right away, or go back to the menu.

Remove Ads & Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase processed by the App Store or Google Play. Inverse does not receive or store your payment-card details.

  • Buy it: tap REMOVE ADS in the main menu, or the small × above the banner during a run.
  • Restore the purchase: open Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same store account you bought it with.
  • Ads still showing after purchase: try Restore Purchases first, then restart the game. If ads remain, contact us.
  • Billing or refund request: contact the store that processed the purchase, because billing is handled by that store.

Ads & Privacy Choices

  • Tracking on iOS: change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ads personalization on Android: reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google or privacy settings.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland: the consent message appears where required; contact us if you need help changing your choice.
  • Inappropriate ad: email us a description or screenshot of the ad and the time it appeared, so we can report or block it.

Best Score

Your best score is saved only on your device. It is not synced between devices and is lost when the app is uninstalled. To start over, use Settings → Reset Best (tap twice to confirm). This cannot be undone.

Language, Sound & Effects

Inverse is available in 10 languages. Change the language, sound effects, music, haptics, screen shake, and glitch effects in Settings. The game follows your device language on first launch. If flashes or motion feel uncomfortable, turn off screen shake and glitch effects.

Technical Problems

  • Make sure you are using the latest version of Inverse from the store.
  • Restart the game, and restart your device if the problem continues.
  • If the game crashes, stutters, or a tap does not register, email us your device details and what was happening on screen.
