Privacy Policy

Inverse · Last updated October 1, 2026

Inverse is a single-player, one-tap arcade game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no leaderboards, and no chat, and we do not ask for your name, email, or any other personal details. Your best score and settings are stored only on your device. Inverse is free to play and shows ads from Google AdMob; AdMob processes device and advertising data to serve and measure those ads, and on iOS it uses your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads. We do not sell your data.

Information We Collect

Information We Do Not Collect

Inverse does not require or offer an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or health data, and the game has no online leaderboards, chat, or user-generated content.

Best Score & Settings (On Your Device)

The game saves your best score, your language, and your sound effects, music, haptics, screen shake, and glitch effects preferences. To decide when full-screen ads may appear, it also keeps a count of how many times you have opened the game. This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. It stays until you reset your best score in the game or uninstall the app.

Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

Inverse shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and occasional full-screen ads after a run ends, provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To deliver, limit, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; the Advertising ID on Android) and other identifiers for the app on your device;
  • your IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • ad interactions and in-app usage related to ads, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information about the ad SDK;
  • technical information such as device model, operating-system version, and language.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies, described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your advertising identifier or IP address in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad reports.

Purchases

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase. It is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play; your payment details are handled entirely by those stores and never reach us. The game receives only confirmation that the purchase was made and stores that result on your device so the ads stay off. Restore Purchases asks the store to confirm earlier purchases.

Consent & Tracking Choices

  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS). On iPhone and iPad, Inverse asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still appear but are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Where required by law, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before ads are requested, so you can choose whether your data may be used for personalized ads. If you decline, limited or non-personalized ads may be shown.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Google or privacy settings.
  • No ads at all. The Remove Ads purchase turns off banner and full-screen ads in the game.

Device Permissions

The only permission Inverse may request is App Tracking Transparency on iOS, described above. Inverse does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or health data. Haptic feedback uses your device's vibration and can be turned off in the game's settings.

How We Use Information

  • Save your best score and settings on your device;
  • Show ads that keep the game free, through Google AdMob, according to your consent and tracking choices;
  • Turn off ads and restore your Remove Ads purchase;
  • Understand aggregated ad performance through AdMob reports.

We do not build profiles about you, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — serves and measures ads and, where required, shows the consent message (User Messaging Platform).
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — process the Remove Ads purchase and restore it.
  • Unity — the game engine and its in-app purchasing library, which pass the purchase between the game and the store.

Inverse contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own. The privacy policy and terms pages open in your device's in-app browser, which follows your browser's own privacy settings. Apple's and Google's operating systems may collect their own diagnostic data under their respective privacy policies.

Data Storage & Security

We do not operate servers that store your data. Your best score and settings stay on your device. Advertising data is transmitted over encrypted connections to Google and handled under Google's security controls.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Reset your best score at any time in Settings → Reset Best.
  • Uninstalling Inverse removes all game data stored on your device.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies. You can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings and manage Google data at myaccount.google.com.

Children's Privacy

Inverse is a general-audience arcade game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been processed through the ads in Inverse, contact us and we will help you with the request.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Allow or deny tracking on iOS, and change it later in your device settings;
  • Give or withhold consent for personalized ads where the consent message applies;
  • Reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ads personalization on your device;
  • Remove all ads with a one-time purchase;
  • Reset your best score or uninstall the app to delete your game data;
  • Contact us at the address below with any privacy question or request.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law (such as access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data). Because we do not hold personal data that identifies you, many requests are best directed to Google for advertising data; we will help where we can.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will revise the date at the top of this page. Please review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how Inverse handles data, you can reach us at business@ketcapp.com.
