Privacy Policy

Lumorse: Morse Code & Light · Last updated July 28, 2026

Lumorse works entirely on your device. It is fully offline — there is no account, no advertising identifier, and no analytics. When you receive a light signal, the camera is used only to measure changes in brightness; video frames are analyzed on-device to decode the message and are never recorded, stored, or uploaded. The rear torch and screen are used to transmit signals. Your saved signals, recent activity, and practice progress stay on your device and never leave it.

Information We Collect

Lumorse is designed to collect as little information as possible. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app does not require you to create an account.

The following data is stored only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Settings — your preferences, such as your default transmission method (flashlight, screen, or haptics), default speed and repeat count, transmission countdown, receiver preferences (automatic threshold, decode speed, waveform display, and whether to keep decoded history), utility-light automatic shutoff, your last used screen-light color and brightness, theme, keep-awake, haptics, safety-warning and photosensitivity acknowledgments, and onboarding completion.
  • Saved signals — the text messages, custom light patterns, and decoded received messages you choose to save to your library, along with their names, creation date, and favorite status.
  • Recent activity — a short list of the signals you have transmitted most recently.
  • Practice progress — your Morse practice sessions completed, correct and total answers, and best accuracy.

We do not collect your name, email, location, usage data, advertising identifiers, photos, videos, messages, or any other personal information.

Device Permissions

The app requests a few permissions to provide its features. Each is used only on your device.

  • Camera — used for two purposes, both entirely on-device: (1) to receive and decode light/Morse signals by measuring changes in brightness in the live camera view, and (2) to control the rear flashlight (torch) when transmitting. The app reads the camera's brightness (luminance) only and never captures, previews, records, or stores any photos or videos. No image data ever leaves your device.
  • Display brightness — used temporarily to brighten the screen for the screen-light feature and for screen-based signal transmission. Your previous brightness level is always restored when you leave the feature or the app.
  • Battery — read only to show your battery percentage, charging state, and low-power status, and to warn you when the battery is low so a signal is not interrupted unexpectedly. The app never sends battery information anywhere.
  • Haptics — used to transmit signals through vibration and to provide light tactile feedback. This can be turned off in settings.
  • Keep awake — optionally keeps the screen awake while transmitting or receiving so a signal is not interrupted. This can be turned off in settings.
  • Clipboard — optionally lets you paste text into the composer and copy a decoded message out. Lumorse reads or writes the clipboard only when you tap the paste or copy buttons; it never monitors your clipboard in the background.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time through your device's system settings.

How We Use Information

Because all data stays on your device, it is used only to:

  • Remember your preferences and defaults between sessions;
  • Restore your saved signals, recent activity, and last screen-light color and brightness when you return;
  • Decode the light signals you point your camera at;
  • Transmit signals through the torch, screen, and haptics;
  • Track your local Morse practice progress;
  • Respect your choices for theme, haptics, keep-awake, and safety warnings;
  • Indicate when your battery is low so a signal is not interrupted unexpectedly.

Data Storage & Retention

All data is stored locally in your device's app sandbox. We do not operate any servers that receive or store your data. Your data is kept for as long as you use the app and until you choose to delete it.

You can delete individual saved signals from your library, or remove everything by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling Lumorse permanently deletes all locally stored data.

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data with any third parties. Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share.

Third-Party Services

Lumorse does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. The app runs on Apple's iOS and Google's Android platforms, which may collect diagnostic data under their own privacy policies. Lumorse has no control over data collected by the operating system.

Children's Privacy

Lumorse is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Your Rights & Choices

Because your data lives only on your device, you have full control over it. You can:

  • View and change your preferences at any time in settings;
  • Delete saved signals from your library at any time;
  • Turn off haptics and keep-awake in settings;
  • Adjust permissions via your device's system settings;
  • Erase all data by uninstalling the app.

Security

Your data is stored within your device's protected app sandbox, isolated from other apps by the operating system. Because your data never travels over a network, it is not exposed to the risks of online transmission. We recommend using your device's built-in security features, such as a passcode or biometric lock, for additional protection.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will revise the date at the top of this page. Please review this page periodically.
