Support & Contact

ARC: Thread · Last updated October 2, 2026

Need help with ARC: Thread? Contact us at business@ketcapp.com. We can help with purchases, coins and lives, leaderboards, ads and privacy choices, and technical problems.

Contact Support

Email: business@ketcapp.com

When contacting us, include a short description of the problem and, when relevant, your device model, operating-system version, ARC: Thread app version (shown at the bottom of Settings), your leaderboard display name, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, Apple ID or Google passwords, or other authentication secrets.

How to Play

  • Start a run from the map. ARC moves forward on its own.
  • Hold anywhere on the screen to steer ARC to the right, and release to steer it to the left. ARC carries momentum, so start turning early.
  • Weave through the gaps in moving and closing gates. Touching a hazard ends the run; the edges of the screen are safe.
  • Thread through narrow safe routes to build your Thread combo and score more.
  • After a crash you can Play On once per run with coins, keeping your score.
  • Complete runs to earn coins and experience, unlock new regions on the map, and earn achievements.

Lives, Coins & Daily Rewards

  • Lives regenerate over time. You can also get a life by watching an optional rewarded ad, or play without limits for a while with Unlimited Lives.
  • Coins come from runs, daily rewards, and coin packs, and are spent on cosmetics and Play On.
  • Daily rewards can be claimed once a day; claim them on consecutive days to keep your streak. Changing your device's date or time does not grant extra rewards and may delay them.
  • Coins, lives, cosmetics, and progress are saved only on your device. They are not synced between devices and are lost when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. ARC: Thread does not receive or store your payment-card details. You can buy coin packs, Remove Ads, Unlimited Lives, and cosmetic bundles in the Shop.

  • Restore purchases: open Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same store account you bought them with. Remove Ads and cosmetic bundles can be restored; coins and Unlimited Lives cannot.
  • Item missing after purchase: make sure you are online, restart the game, then try Restore Purchases. If the item is still missing, email us the purchase date and the store's order number.
  • Remove Ads: turns off the full-screen ads after runs. Optional rewarded ads stay available.
  • Billing or refund request: contact the store that processed the purchase, because billing is handled by that store.

Leaderboards & Daily Challenge

  • Leaderboards have Daily, Weekly, All Time, and Challenge tabs. Every player gets the same level in the Daily Challenge each day.
  • Scores are submitted when a run ends and you are online. If your score is missing, check your connection and play another run.
  • You appear under a generated display name (such as "PLAYER 7F3A"). Reinstalling the game gives you a new display name.
  • To have your leaderboard entries deleted, email us your display name and the approximate dates of your runs.

Ads & Privacy Choices

  • Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland: change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Tracking on iOS: change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.
  • Ads personalization on Android: reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google or privacy settings.
  • Rewarded ad gave no reward: the reward is granted only when the ad is watched to the end. If an ad was completed but nothing arrived, email us the time it happened.
  • Inappropriate ad: email us a description or screenshot of the ad and the time it appeared, so we can report or block it.

Notifications

ARC: Thread can remind you when a daily reward is ready, when your lives are full, and when a new Daily Challenge starts. Turn reminders on or off in Settings. If they stay off, allow notifications for ARC: Thread in your device settings.

Language, Sound & Vibration

ARC: Thread is available in 10 languages. Change the language, music volume, sound effects volume, and vibration in Settings. The game follows your device language on first launch.

Technical Problems

  • Make sure you are using the latest version of ARC: Thread from the store.
  • Restart the game, and restart your device if the problem continues.
  • If the game crashes, stutters, or does not respond to your touch, email us your device details and what was happening on screen.
