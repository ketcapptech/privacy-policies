Privacy Policy

ARC: Thread · Last updated October 2, 2026

ARC: Thread is a single-player, one-touch arcade game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no chat, and we do not ask for your name, email, or any other personal details. Your progress, coins, cosmetics, and settings are stored only on your device. To run the online leaderboards, the game signs in anonymously with Google Firebase and sends your scores under a generated display name. ARC: Thread is free to play and shows ads from Google AdMob; AdMob processes device and advertising data to serve and measure those ads, according to your consent and tracking choices. Optional in-app purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. Reminder notifications are created on your device and only if you allow them. We do not sell your data.

Information We Collect

Information We Do Not Collect

ARC: Thread does not require or offer an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or health data, and the game has no chat or user-generated content.

Game Progress & Settings (On Your Device)

The game saves the following data only on your device. Apart from the leaderboard data described below, it is never sent to us:

  • your best score, map and region progress, experience level, achievements, and gameplay statistics;
  • your coins, lives, unlocked and equipped cosmetics, and daily reward streak;
  • your music and sound effects volume, vibration, notifications, and language preferences;
  • a count of how many times you have opened the game, used to decide when full-screen ads may appear;
  • the latest date and time the game has seen, used so that changing the device clock cannot claim extra daily rewards or lives;
  • a record of purchases already delivered, so a purchase is not delivered twice.

This data stays until you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

Leaderboards (Google Firebase)

ARC: Thread has online daily, weekly, all-time, and Daily Challenge leaderboards, run on Google Firebase. To take part without an account, the game signs in with Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which creates a random user ID that is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or store account. The game also generates a display name (such as "PLAYER 7F3A"), which is shown to other players next to your scores.

When a run ends, the game sends the following to our Firebase backend:

  • your anonymous user ID and display name;
  • the run's score, distance, duration, number of threads cleared, run seed, and whether it was a Daily Challenge run;
  • the time the run was submitted.

We use this data only to rank scores, show the leaderboards, and check that a score is plausible for the run that produced it, so cheated scores can be rejected. When the game talks to Firebase, Google may also process technical data such as your IP address, device and operating-system information, and Firebase installation identifiers, to provide and secure the service. Leaderboards work only while you are online; if you are offline, your run is simply not submitted.

Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

ARC: Thread shows occasional full-screen ads after a run ends (never during your first session) and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example to get an extra life or unlock a cosmetic. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To deliver, limit, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; the Advertising ID on Android) and other identifiers for the app on your device;
  • your IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • ad interactions and in-app usage related to ads, such as ad views, taps, and completed rewarded ads;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information about the ad SDK;
  • technical information such as device model, operating-system version, and language.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies, described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your advertising identifier or IP address in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad reports.

Purchases

ARC: Thread offers optional in-app purchases: coin packs, Remove Ads, Unlimited Lives for a limited time, and cosmetic bundles. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play; your payment details are handled entirely by those stores and never reach us. The game receives only confirmation that a purchase was made and stores the result on your device so the item stays delivered. Restore Purchases asks the store to confirm earlier non-consumable purchases.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device, for example when a daily reward is ready, when your streak is about to end, when your lives are full again, when a new Daily Challenge is available, or when you have not played for a while. These reminders are created by the app itself; they are not push notifications, and no notification token or data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device settings.

Consent & Tracking Choices

  • Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Where required by law, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before ads are requested, so you can choose whether your data may be used for personalized ads. If you decline, limited or non-personalized ads may be shown. Where this applies, you can review your choice at any time with Settings → Privacy Options in the game.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS). On iPhone and iPad, ARC: Thread asks for permission, through Google's consent flow, before your advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still appear but are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Google or privacy settings.
  • Fewer ads. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the full-screen ads shown after runs. Rewarded ads stay available because you choose when to watch them.

Device Permissions

ARC: Thread may ask for permission to send notifications and, on iOS, for App Tracking Transparency, as described above. The game uses an internet connection for ads, purchases, and leaderboards. ARC: Thread does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or health data. The game checks whether other audio is already playing on your device only so it does not interrupt your own music. Vibration uses your device's haptics and can be turned off in the game's settings.

How We Use Information

  • Save your progress, coins, cosmetics, and settings on your device;
  • Rank your scores on the online leaderboards, show your display name to other players, and run the Daily Challenge;
  • Check submitted scores and reject cheated or implausible ones;
  • Send local reminder notifications, if you allow them;
  • Show ads that keep the game free, through Google AdMob, according to your consent and tracking choices, and give you the reward for rewarded ads you watch;
  • Deliver and restore your in-app purchases;
  • Understand aggregated ad performance through AdMob reports.

We do not build profiles about you, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — serves and measures ads and, where required, shows the consent message (User Messaging Platform) and the App Tracking Transparency request.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — Anonymous Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions store and check leaderboard scores. See the Firebase privacy information.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — process in-app purchases and restore them.
  • Unity — the game engine, its in-app purchasing library, which passes purchases between the game and the store, and its mobile notifications library, which schedules local reminders on your device.

ARC: Thread contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own. The privacy policy and terms pages open in your device's in-app browser, which follows your browser's own privacy settings. Apple's and Google's operating systems may collect their own diagnostic data under their respective privacy policies.

Data Storage & Security

Your progress and settings stay on your device and are not backed up by us. Leaderboard data is stored in our Google Firebase project, on Google Cloud servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States; Google provides safeguards for such transfers under its data processing terms. Leaderboard and advertising data are transmitted over encrypted connections, and access to the leaderboard backend is restricted by Firebase security rules.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Uninstalling ARC: Thread, or clearing its data in your device settings, removes all game data stored on your device. A new installation signs in with a new anonymous user ID.
  • Daily, weekly, and Daily Challenge scores are kept only as long as needed to show those boards and their recent history; all-time scores are kept while the game's leaderboards are active. To have your leaderboard entries deleted, email us your display name and the approximate dates of your runs; we will remove the matching entries.
  • Non-consumable purchases can be restored on a new installation with Settings → Restore Purchases; coins and other consumed items cannot be restored.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies. You can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings and manage Google data at myaccount.google.com.

Children's Privacy

ARC: Thread is a general-audience arcade game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been processed through the ads in ARC: Thread, contact us and we will help you with the request.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Give or withhold consent for personalized ads where the consent message applies, and review it later in Settings → Privacy Options;
  • Allow or deny tracking on iOS, and change it later in your device settings;
  • Reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ads personalization on your device;
  • Turn notifications on or off in the game or in your device settings;
  • Turn off full-screen ads with the Remove Ads purchase;
  • Uninstall the app or clear its data to delete your game data;
  • Ask us to delete your leaderboard entries;
  • Contact us at the address below with any privacy question or request.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law (such as access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data). The only data we hold is your pseudonymous leaderboard data, which we can find from your display name. Requests about advertising data are best directed to Google; we will help where we can.
