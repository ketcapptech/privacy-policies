Privacy Policy

Flashlight: Strobe & SOS · Last updated July 16, 2026

Flashlight: Strobe & SOS works entirely on your device. It is fully offline — there is no account, no analytics, and no backend. The camera is used only to switch on your phone's rear light, and no photos or videos are ever captured or recorded. Your preferences are saved locally on your phone and never leave it.

Information We Collect

Flashlight: Strobe & SOS is designed to collect as little information as possible. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app does not require you to create an account.

The following data is stored only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Settings — your preferences, such as your default mode, default automatic shutoff, theme, keep-awake, haptics, safety-warning acknowledgment, onboarding completion, and your last used screen-light color and brightness.

We do not collect your name, email, location, usage data, advertising identifiers, photos, videos, or any other personal information.

Device Permissions

The app requests a few permissions to provide its features. Each is used only on your device.

  • Camera — required only to turn your phone's rear flashlight on and off. The app does not capture, preview, or store any photos or videos; the camera is used exclusively to control the torch.
  • Display brightness — used temporarily to brighten the screen for the full-screen light feature. Your previous brightness level is always restored when you leave the feature or the app.
  • Battery — read only to show your battery percentage, charging state, and low-power status, and to warn you when the battery is low. The app never sends battery information anywhere.
  • Haptics — to provide light tactile feedback. This can be turned off in settings.
  • Keep awake — optionally keeps the screen awake while the app is in use so a signal is not interrupted. This can be turned off in settings.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time through your device's system settings.

How We Use Information

Because all data stays on your device, it is used only to:

  • Remember your preferences between sessions;
  • Restore your last screen-light color and brightness when you return;
  • Show your default flashlight mode and automatic-shutoff timer;
  • Respect your choices for theme, haptics, keep-awake, and safety warnings;
  • Indicate when your battery is low so a signal is not interrupted unexpectedly.

Data Storage & Retention

All data is stored locally in your device's app sandbox. We do not operate any servers that receive or store your data. Your data is kept for as long as you use the app and until you choose to delete it.

You can reset individual preferences in the app's settings at any time, or remove everything by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling Flashlight: Strobe & SOS permanently deletes all locally stored data.

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data with any third parties. Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share.

Third-Party Services

Flashlight: Strobe & SOS does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. The app runs on Apple's iOS and Google's Android platforms, which may collect diagnostic data under their own privacy policies. Flashlight: Strobe & SOS has no control over data collected by the operating system.

Children's Privacy

Flashlight: Strobe & SOS is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Your Rights & Choices

Because your data lives only on your device, you have full control over it. You can:

  • View and change your preferences at any time in settings;
  • Turn off haptics and keep-awake in settings;
  • Adjust permissions via your device's system settings;
  • Erase all data by uninstalling the app.

Security

Your data is stored within your device's protected app sandbox, isolated from other apps by the operating system. Because your data never travels over a network, it is not exposed to the risks of online transmission. We recommend using your device's built-in security features, such as a passcode or biometric lock, for additional protection.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will revise the date at the top of this page. Please review this page periodically.
