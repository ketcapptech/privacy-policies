Privacy Policy

Runiva: Run & Walk · Last updated October 26, 2026

Runiva is a social running and walking app. To use it, you create an account by signing in with Apple, Google, or email. Runiva records your GPS location only while you are recording a run or walk — never in the background of your daily life. Every new activity is private by default, and activities or records you make public never include your exact route coordinates. Runiva shows no ads, uses no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or sell your data. You can delete your account and its data at any time, directly in the app.

Information We Collect

Account & Profile Information

An account is required to use Runiva. When you sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we receive from your chosen provider your name, email address, and profile photo (if the provider shares one). You can optionally add a username, display name, bio, home city, and links to your public social media profiles.

During setup, we ask for your date of birth to confirm that you are at least 13 years old and, for runners aged 13–17, to apply extra safety protections (see Children & Teens). It is stored with your account so these checks stay consistent across your devices, and you can view or update it in Edit Profile. Your date of birth is kept private, is used for nothing else, and is never shown on or copied to your public profile.

Body & Fitness Profile

You can optionally provide your gender, height, weight, running goal, and experience level. Height and weight can be imported from Apple Health — but only when you tap the import button. Your weight is used to estimate calories burned, and these values help tailor training feedback.

Location Data

  • Only while recording. While you are actively recording a run or walk, Runiva captures precise GPS route points (coordinates, timestamps, distance, and altitude) to calculate your distance, pace, splits, and route. Location tracking stops when you end the activity.
  • Background tracking of an activity in progress. With your permission ("Always" on iOS, background location on Android), Runiva can keep recording only the activity in progress while your screen is locked or the app is in the background. This is an optional setting you can turn off at any time.
  • Start point naming. The name of the place where your activity starts (for example, a park or district) is determined on your device.
  • Live challenges. If you take part in a live race and enable live sharing, your live position is shared only with your opponent, only for the duration of the race. This is off unless you turn it on.
  • Nearby discovery. If you opt in to discovering runners and Echoes nearby, your location is first coarsened on your device to an area of roughly 1 km, and only that approximate area is shared. Your exact location is never used for discovery.
  • Public Echoes. When you publish an Echo, its approximate start area (rounded to roughly 1 km) is published with it so nearby runners can find it. The exact route and start point are never published.

Every new activity is private by default. Activities, records, and Echoes that you choose to make public contain your results (distance, duration, pace, elevation) but never your exact route coordinates.

Health & Fitness Data

Runiva stores your workout metrics — distance, duration, pace, splits, elevation, estimated energy burn, and personal records. If you allow it in Apple Health, Runiva can also write your completed running workouts to Apple Health, and can read your height and weight only when you explicitly import them.

If you allow it, Runiva reads your heart rate from Apple Health while you record a run and stores a heart rate summary with that run (average and maximum heart rate, and time spent in each heart rate zone). This summary is part of your private run, is never included in public runs, and is deleted with the run or your account. To calculate your heart rate zones, Runiva reads your date of birth from Apple Health; it is used only on your device and is not uploaded.

Data Runiva reads from Apple Health (HealthKit) is used only to provide Runiva's fitness features. It is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties. It is not stored in iCloud.

Social Content

Content you create in the app — shared runs, Echoes (run challenges you publish), comments, kudos, follows, club memberships, and challenge results — is stored so the social features work, and is visible to other runners only according to your privacy choices for each item.

Reports & Blocks

If you report a runner or a piece of content, we store the report (the reason, the reported account and content, and a short excerpt of the reported text) and send it by email to our moderation inbox so it can be reviewed within 24 hours. If you block a runner, we store that block so it can be enforced on our servers. To keep the community safe, text you write (such as names, usernames, bios, comments, and Echo messages) is checked on your device against a list of objectionable words before it is published.

Device & Technical Data

To deliver push notifications (for example, when someone follows you, joins your club, or beats your Echo), we store your device's push notification token (from Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging), your platform, and your language. Runiva includes no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Purchases

Runiva Pro is an optional subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play; payment details are handled entirely by those stores and never reach us. We receive only your subscription status (through our subscription provider, RevenueCat) so the app knows which features are unlocked.

Device Permissions

  • Location (While Using / Always on iOS; location on Android) — to record your route, distance, and pace during a run. The "Always" permission is used solely to continue an in-progress run when the screen is locked; you can disable background tracking in Runiva's settings and revoke permissions in your device settings at any time.
  • Precise location — requested temporarily during outdoor runs so distance and pace are calculated accurately.
  • Apple Health — to save completed workouts you choose to save; to read your height and weight only when you import them; and, during a run, to read your heart rate and your date of birth to calculate heart rate zones. Your date of birth from Health is used only on your device and is not uploaded. You control this in iOS Settings > Health.
  • Notifications — to send you the push notifications described above. Every notification category can be turned off individually in the app, and you can turn off notifications entirely in your device settings.
  • Photo library (add only) — used only to save run share cards you create. Runiva never reads, scans, or uploads your photos.
  • Android only: foreground service — while a run is in progress, an ongoing notification keeps tracking alive; it disappears when the run ends.

Runiva does not request access to your camera, microphone, or contacts.

How We Use Information

  • Create and secure your account and sync your runs across your devices;
  • Record your runs and calculate distance, pace, splits, elevation, energy estimates, personal records, and progress toward your goal;
  • Provide the social features you enable — sharing runs, Echoes, clubs, challenges, follows, kudos, and comments — with the audience you choose;
  • Run live challenges and, if you opt in, share your live position with your opponent during the race;
  • Power optional nearby discovery using only coarsened (~1 km) location;
  • Send the push notifications you have enabled, in your language;
  • Recognize your Runiva Pro subscription entitlement;
  • Save workouts to Apple Health when you choose to;
  • Review reports, enforce blocks, and keep the community safe, including the extra protections for runners aged 13–17.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or selling, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

Sharing & Public Content

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data leaves your account only as follows:

  • Content you choose to share — public runs, records, Echoes, profile information, and comments are visible to other signed-in Runiva users. Public runs and records never include your exact route.
  • Live races — your live position goes only to your opponent, only during the race, only if you enable live sharing.
  • Service providers — the third-party services listed below that operate our authentication, database, push delivery, subscriptions, maps, and moderation email. They process data only to provide their service to us, and we rely on them to protect it to at least the standard described in this policy;
  • Legal — if we are ever required to disclose information by law, we would do so only to the extent necessary.

Third-Party Services

Runiva relies on a small set of services, each used for a specific purpose:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication, app database (Firestore), and delivery of push notifications to Android devices. Your run and profile data is stored in Firestore under Google's security controls.
  • Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In — used only to authenticate you; Runiva receives your name, email, and profile photo, not your credentials.
  • Apple Push Notification service & Firebase Cloud Messaging — deliver push notifications to your device.
  • RevenueCat — manages Runiva Pro subscription status across platforms.
  • Email delivery (Firebase “Trigger Email” over our mail provider) — delivers content reports to our moderation inbox.
  • Apple Maps (iOS) and Google Maps (Android) — display your route on the map. The map provider processes what it needs to serve map imagery (for example, your IP address and the visible map region) under its own privacy policy.
  • Apple Health — stores workouts, shares your height and weight with Runiva on request, and, if you allow it, your heart rate and date of birth during a run. You remain in full control of this connection in iOS Settings.

Runiva contains no advertising networks and no third-party analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. Apple's and Google's operating systems may collect their own diagnostic data under their respective privacy policies.

Data Storage & Security

Your account data is stored in Google Cloud (Firestore), and our server functions run in Google Cloud's europe-west1 (Belgium) region. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, and security rules ensure that private data (your runs, settings, and personal details) is readable only by you within the app; other users cannot see it. Content you explicitly make public is readable by other signed-in users. Runiva staff do not browse your private data. Access is limited to a small number of authorized personnel and occurs only when needed to operate the service (for example, automated server functions that process your runs), to respond to a support request you make, to investigate abuse or security issues, or to comply with legal obligations. Run and workout history is also kept on your device for offline use and fast display.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Runiva under Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, runs, Echoes, public records, attempts, comments, kudos, follows, clubs you own and your club memberships, reports you made, runners you blocked, notifications, and push tokens. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Runiva's Sign in with Apple access. Two exceptions: finished live challenges you completed against another runner may be retained as part of that opponent's own history, without your route data; and report emails already delivered to our moderation inbox are kept as long as needed to resolve the report and protect other users. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device.

Children & Teens

Runiva is not directed at children under 13. During setup, users enter their date of birth, and anyone under 13 cannot complete setup or use the app. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it. Parents or guardians who believe a child has created an account can contact us and we will remove it.

Runners aged 13–17 get extra protections: they cannot make themselves visible in Nearby discovery, and live challenges are limited to runners they follow and who follow them back. Our servers read a teen's private date of birth only to decline challenge requests from people they do not know.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Review and edit your profile, body metrics, and goals at any time in the app;
  • Keep every activity private, or choose activity by activity what to share — public activities never expose your exact route;
  • Turn background tracking, live location sharing, and nearby discovery on or off in settings;
  • Customize or disable each type of push notification in settings;
  • Control Health access in iOS Settings > Health, and revoke location or photo permissions in your device settings at any time;
  • Delete individual runs or Echoes you no longer want;
  • Delete your entire account and its data directly in the app;
  • Contact us at the address below with any privacy question or request.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law (such as access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data). We will honor these requests to the extent applicable.
