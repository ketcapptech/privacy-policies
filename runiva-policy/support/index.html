Account & Sign-In Help

Runiva supports sign-in methods such as Apple, Google, and email. If you are unable to sign in, first make sure you are using the same sign-in method that was used when the account was created.

If the problem continues, contact us and tell us which sign-in method you use. Never send us your Apple, Google, or email password.

Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Runiva account directly in the app under Settings → Account → Delete Account. The app may ask you to sign in again before deletion for security reasons.

Details about what is deleted and what limited information may remain are described in our Privacy Policy. If you cannot access the app and need help with an account deletion request, email business@ketcapp.com.

Subscriptions & Runiva Pro

Runiva Pro purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. Runiva does not receive or store your payment-card details.

  • Restore a purchase: use the Restore Purchases option in Runiva where available.
  • Cancel or manage a subscription: use your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Billing or refund request: contact the store that processed the purchase, because billing is handled by that store.
  • Pro access missing after purchase: try Restore Purchases first, then contact us if the entitlement is still missing.

Deleting your Runiva account or uninstalling Runiva does not automatically cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription.

Run Recording & GPS Problems

If a run or walk is not recording correctly, check the following:

  • Location permission is enabled for Runiva.
  • Precise location is enabled when accurate outdoor tracking is needed.
  • If you want recording to continue with the screen locked, background location access is enabled.
  • Battery-saving or operating-system restrictions are not preventing location updates.
  • You have a clear enough GPS signal for outdoor route tracking.

GPS measurements may vary because of buildings, trees, tunnels, weather, device hardware, satellite conditions, or operating-system behavior.

Apple Health

On supported iOS devices, Runiva can write completed workouts to Apple Health and can read height and weight when you explicitly choose to import them. You control these permissions from your iPhone's Health and privacy settings.

If a Health feature is not working, verify that Runiva has the required Health permissions and that the relevant data type is available on your device.

Notifications

Runiva may send notifications for social and activity-related events you have enabled. You can customize notification categories in Runiva and can disable app notifications entirely in your device settings.

Report a User, Content, or Safety Issue

If you encounter abusive, threatening, harassing, fraudulent, unsafe, or otherwise inappropriate behavior or content in Runiva, use any available in-app reporting controls. You can also contact us at business@ketcapp.com and include enough information for us to identify the relevant account, content, run, club, comment, or challenge.

Do not use Runiva to report an emergency. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service.
