Privacy Policy

ChromaPin · Last updated July 15, 2026

ChromaPin is privacy-first. All color analysis happens on your device. The app never records video, never uploads your camera frames or photos, and never sends your colors anywhere. There is no account and no analytics.

Information We Collect

ChromaPin is designed to collect as little information as possible. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app does not require you to create an account.

The following data is stored only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Saved colors — colors you choose to save, including their values, timestamp, an optional name, and an optional reference image.
  • Settings — your preferences, such as sampling size, copy format, appearance, and haptics.

We do not collect your name, email, location, usage data, advertising identifiers, or any other personal information.

Device Permissions

ChromaPin requests a few permissions to provide its features. Each is used only on your device.

  • Camera — to identify colors from the live preview. The camera feed is processed in memory and is never recorded or uploaded.
  • Photos — to pick colors from existing photos in your library. Selected images stay on your device.
  • Clipboard — to copy a color value when you choose to. The app only writes to the clipboard and never reads it.
  • Haptics — to provide light tactile feedback. This can be turned off in settings.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time through your device's system settings.

How We Use Information

Because all data stays on your device, it is used only to:

  • Display your saved colors and their details;
  • Remember your preferences between sessions;
  • Optionally keep a reference image alongside a saved color;
  • Copy color values to your clipboard at your request;
  • Share a color or image with other apps on your device at your explicit request.

Data Storage & Retention

All data is stored locally in your device's app sandbox. We do not operate any servers that receive or store your data. Your data is kept for as long as you use the app and until you choose to delete it.

You can delete individual saved colors at any time, or remove everything by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling ChromaPin permanently deletes all locally stored data.

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data with any third parties. Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share.

The app includes a share function that lets you send a color or image to other apps on your device through your operating system's native sharing sheet. Once shared, that content is handled by the app you select — ChromaPin does not control or retain it.

Third-Party Services

ChromaPin does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. The app runs on Apple's iOS and Google's Android platforms, which may collect diagnostic data under their own privacy policies. ChromaPin has no control over data collected by the operating system.

Children's Privacy

ChromaPin is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Your Rights & Choices

Because your data lives only on your device, you have full control over it. You can:

  • View your saved colors and settings at any time;
  • Delete individual saved colors whenever you want;
  • Adjust permissions via your device's system settings;
  • Turn off haptics and the "Save original image" option in settings;
  • Erase all data by uninstalling the app.

Security

Your data is stored within your device's protected app sandbox, isolated from other apps by the operating system. Because your data never travels over a network, it is not exposed to the risks of online transmission. We recommend using your device's built-in security features, such as a passcode or biometric lock, for additional protection.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will revise the date at the top of this page. Please review this page periodically.
