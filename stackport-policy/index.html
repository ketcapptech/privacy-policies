Privacy Policy

Stackport · Last updated November 12, 2026

Stackport is a single-player port puzzle game. It has no accounts of its own and no chat, and we do not ask for your name, email, or any other personal details. Your progress is saved on your device and backed up to your own iCloud or Google Play Games account. Stackport is free to play: it shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases. We use Google Firebase to understand how the game is played, to find crashes, and to tune the game remotely. On iOS, your advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking. We do not sell your data.

Information We Collect

Information We Do Not Collect

Stackport does not require or offer an account of its own. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or health data, and the game has no chat or user-generated content.

Game Progress & Settings (On Your Device)

The game saves your level, wins, coins, boosters, hearts, port buildings, win streak, piggy bank, daily rewards, and which tutorials you have seen, together with your language and your music, sound, and haptics preferences. This data is stored on your device and is never sent to our own servers. Your settings stay only on the device.

Progress Backup (iCloud, Keychain & Google Play Games)

So that you do not lose your progress when you reinstall the game or change phones, Stackport backs up a copy of your game progress (not your settings) to services tied to your own Apple or Google account:

  • iOS: your device's Keychain and iCloud key-value storage, on the Apple ID signed in on your device;
  • Android: a Google Play Games saved game, on the Google account you sign in to Play Games with.

The backup contains only game data such as your level, coins, and boosters. It is stored by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, and we cannot see or access it. When a backup is further along than the progress on your device, the game restores it.

Game Center & Google Play Games

On iOS, Stackport signs in to Apple Game Center if you use it; on Android, it signs in to Google Play Games if you allow it. The game reports the highest level you have reached to a leaderboard, where other players can see it with your Game Center or Play Games nickname and avatar. Apple and Google handle your gaming profile under their own policies; the game itself does not read or store your name, email, or friends list. You can sign out of, or turn off, Game Center or Play Games in your device settings at any time.

Gameplay Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players move through the game, so we can balance levels and fix what does not work. It records game events such as levels started, won, lost, or quit; tutorials seen; boosters used; buildings upgraded; shop visits and purchases made; and rewarded ads watched. Firebase also collects:

  • an app instance identifier for the game on your device, and on iOS the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (the Advertising ID on Android);
  • your approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address;
  • technical information such as device model, operating-system version, language, app version, and session length.

We see this data only in aggregated reports and do not use it to identify you.

Crash Reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report containing the error details, device model, operating-system version, app version, free memory and disk space, and a random installation identifier. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs.

Remote Settings & Push Messages (Firebase Remote Config & Cloud Messaging)

Firebase Remote Config lets us adjust game settings, such as difficulty and offers, without an app update. Firebase Cloud Messaging registers a push token for your device so that we can send game notifications. Both use a random Firebase installation identifier and do not identify you.

Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

Stackport shows a banner ad, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for in-game rewards. These ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To deliver, limit, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; the Advertising ID on Android) and other identifiers for the app on your device;
  • your IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • ad interactions and in-app usage related to ads, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information about the ad SDK;
  • technical information such as device model, operating-system version, and language.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies, described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your advertising identifier or IP address in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad reports.

Purchases

Stackport offers optional in-app purchases: coin packs, booster packs, a starter pack, piggy bank coins, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase. They are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play; your payment details are handled entirely by those stores and never reach us. The game receives only confirmation that a purchase was made and adds the items to your game progress. Restore Purchases asks the store to confirm an earlier Remove Ads purchase.

Consent & Tracking Choices

  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS). On iPhone and iPad, Stackport asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. It asks before the ad and analytics services start. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still appear but are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Where required by law, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before ads are requested, so you can choose whether your data may be used for personalized ads. If you decline, limited or non-personalized ads may be shown. You can change your choice later with Settings → Privacy Settings in the game.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Google or privacy settings.
  • Fewer ads. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the banner and full-screen ads in the game. Rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch them.

Device Permissions

  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS), described above.
  • Notifications. Stackport may ask to send notifications, for example when your hearts are full again, when a daily reward is ready, or when new levels arrive. These reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Stackport does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or health data. Haptic feedback uses your device's vibration and can be turned off in the game's settings.

How We Use Information

  • Save your game progress and settings on your device, and back up your progress to your own iCloud or Play Games account;
  • Show your highest level on the Game Center or Play Games leaderboard;
  • Show ads that keep the game free, through Google AdMob, according to your consent and tracking choices;
  • Deliver your in-app purchases and restore Remove Ads;
  • Understand how the game is played and balance levels, through aggregated Firebase reports;
  • Find and fix crashes and bugs;
  • Adjust game settings remotely and send game reminders you have allowed.

We do not build profiles about you, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — serves and measures ads and, where required, shows the consent message (User Messaging Platform).
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, and Cloud Messaging, as described above. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Apple (iCloud, Keychain, Game Center, App Store) — back up your progress, host the leaderboard, and process purchases.
  • Google (Play Games, Google Play) — back up your progress, host the leaderboard, and process purchases.
  • Unity — the game engine, its in-app purchasing library, which passes purchases between the game and the store, and its notifications library, which schedules reminders on your device.

The privacy policy and terms pages open in your device's in-app browser, which follows your browser's own privacy settings. The Rate Us button uses Apple's or Google's own review prompt. Apple's and Google's operating systems may collect their own diagnostic data under their respective privacy policies.

Data Storage & Security

We do not operate servers that store your data. Game progress and settings are stored on your device, and the backup copy is stored in your own iCloud or Play Games account. Analytics, crash, and advertising data are transmitted over encrypted connections to Google and handled under Google's security controls; this data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Uninstalling Stackport removes the game data stored in the app on your device. The backup copy remains so that you can get your progress back: on iOS, the iCloud copy can be removed in your iCloud storage settings and the Keychain copy is removed when the device is erased; on Android, it can be removed under Play Games → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google for a limited period set in our Firebase settings, and crash reports for up to 90 days.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies. You can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings and manage Google data at myaccount.google.com.
  • To ask us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your device, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

Stackport is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been processed through Stackport, contact us and we will help you with the request.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Allow or deny tracking on iOS, and change it later in your device settings;
  • Give or withhold consent for personalized ads where the consent message applies, and change it in the game's Privacy Settings;
  • Reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ads personalization on your device;
  • Turn notifications on or off in your device settings;
  • Sign out of Game Center or Play Games, or turn off iCloud for the game;
  • Remove banner and full-screen ads with a one-time purchase;
  • Uninstall the app and delete your backup to remove your game data;
  • Contact us at the address below with any privacy question or request.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law (such as access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data). Because we do not hold personal data that identifies you, many requests are best directed to Apple or Google; we will help where we can.
