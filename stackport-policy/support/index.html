Support & Contact

Stackport · Last updated November 12, 2026

Need help with Stackport? Contact us at business@ketcapp.com. We can help with purchases, ads and privacy choices, game progress and backup, and technical problems.

Contact Support

Email: business@ketcapp.com

When contacting us, include a short description of the problem and, when relevant, your device model, operating-system version, Stackport app version, the level number, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, Apple ID or Google passwords, or other authentication secrets.

How to Play

  • Tap a stack in the yard to lift its top container with the crane.
  • Tap a ship to load the container, and release when the ring turns green so the swinging container lands.
  • No room on a ship yet? Park the container on the apron and load it later.
  • Trucks keep bringing new containers and ships have timers: load every ship before its time runs out, and do not let the yard overflow.
  • Use boosters such as Undo, Magnet, Freeze, and Tug when a level gets tight, and spend the coins you win on your port's buildings.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. Stackport does not receive or store your payment-card details.

  • Restore Remove Ads: open Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same store account you bought it with.
  • Ads still showing after Remove Ads: try Restore Purchases first, then restart the game. Remove Ads turns off the banner and full-screen ads; rewarded ads stay available when you choose to watch them. If other ads remain, contact us.
  • Coins or boosters missing after a purchase: restart the game while connected to the internet. If they still do not appear, email us the time of the purchase and your receipt's order number.
  • Billing or refund request: contact the store that processed the purchase, because billing is handled by that store.

Ads & Privacy Choices

  • Tracking on iOS: change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ads personalization on Android: reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google or privacy settings.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland: the consent message appears where required; change your choice later with Settings → Privacy Settings in the game.
  • Inappropriate ad: email us a description or screenshot of the ad and the time it appeared, so we can report or block it.

Game Progress & Backup

Progress is saved on your device and backed up automatically: to iCloud on iOS, and to Google Play Games on Android once you sign in to Play Games.

  • New phone or reinstall: sign in with the same Apple ID (with iCloud on) or the same Play Games account, then open the game. If your progress has not appeared, close and reopen the game once it has had a moment online.
  • Progress lost: email us your device model and the level you had reached; we will help where we can, but progress without a backup cannot be recovered.

Leaderboard

Your highest level is sent to the Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) leaderboard when you are signed in. To change your nickname or hide your profile, use Game Center or Play Games settings on your device.

Notifications

Stackport can remind you when your hearts are full, when your daily reward is ready, and when new levels arrive. Turn notifications on or off in your device's settings for Stackport.

Language, Sound & Haptics

Stackport is available in 10 languages. Change the language, music, sound, and haptics in Settings. The game follows your device language on first launch.

Technical Problems

  • Make sure you are using the latest version of Stackport from the store.
  • Restart the game, and restart your device if the problem continues.
  • If the game crashes or a level behaves unexpectedly, email us the level number and your device details.
